# Privacy Policy for Bitácora de Viajes

**Last Updated:** November 2026

## Core Privacy Principle
**Bitácora de Viajes** is built on a private, local-first philosophy. Your personal travel memories, diary entries, financial expense records, and photographs belong solely to you and your travel partner.

## 1. Data Storage & Ownership
- **Local Storage**: All trip journals, photos, expense distributions, and numismatic collectibles are stored on your device using Apple's **SwiftData** framework within the secure iOS Application Sandbox.
- **No Remote Account Required**: The application functions without requiring account registration, email verification, or phone numbers.
- **Biometric Security**: App-lock features utilize Apple's `LocalAuthentication` framework (Face ID, Touch ID, or Device Passcode). Biometric verification happens directly within Apple's hardware Secure Enclave; the app never receives or stores your biometric geometry or passwords.

## 2. External Services & Network Usage
- **Weather Information**: The app queries Open-Meteo strictly over encrypted HTTPS to display meteorological forecasts for selected destination coordinates. No personal identity or persistent device identifiers are sent with these requests.
- **Zero Third-Party Trackers**: We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking suites, or monetized analytics platforms.

## 3. Data Deletion
Since all data is held locally on your iOS device or within your private personal iCloud container, you retain full control. Removing the app or wiping app data permanently deletes all records from your device.
